# The Endless Errand

An idle fantasy RPG that plays itself. Roll a hero, pick a class, then sit back and watch them fight, loot, haggle, quest and level up on their own.

## ▶ [Play it in your browser](https://carrandas.github.io/ProgressQ/)

## How it works

1. **Create a hero.** Choose a name, a race (Human, Hill Dwarf, Wood Elf, Halfling, Gnome or Goblin) and a class:
   - **Mage**: reads old books, sets things on fire, apologises afterwards. Favours INT.
   - **Warrior**: hits things until they stop being things. Favours STR.
   - **Thief**: gets in, gets out, gets paid. Favours DEX.
2. **Roll attributes** (3d6 each) as often as you like.
3. **Watch.** Your hero travels to hunting grounds that match their level, fights monsters and fills their pack with loot. When the pack is full, they head back to Kettlewick to sell it and buy better gear.

Along the way they:

- gain levels, raise stats and learn new spells, techniques or tricks
- take on quests that pay out gold, equipment or blessings
- work through a story told in chapters

Every notable moment is written to the **Chronicle**.

## Features

- No install or build step: the whole game is one `index.html` file
- Progress saves automatically in your browser
- Speed controls (1×, 2×, 5×, 25×) to watch the grind go faster
- Works on desktop and phone

## Run it locally

Download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser.
